**The Midnight Vault** — a top-down bank heist game. You are a thief working one
bank floor after dark: lift every piece of loot — two gold bars, two cash stacks
and the gold inside the vault — crack the vault door by holding E beside it, and
slip out to the getaway van. Three guards patrol with vision cones clipped by
walls and desks, so cover is real; two ceiling cameras sweep their cones across
the hall; and a blinking laser grid guards the right side of the room — crossing
an active beam spikes the alarm. Sneak with Shift to raise the alarm half as
fast, toss a coin with Space (or right-click) to pull a guard off his patrol,
and watch the alarm meter: at 100% the bank locks down and the night is over.

One floor, one clean run: loot plus a time bonus for a fast escape. Keyboard and
mouse, synthesized sound effects (WebAudio, no files), fully offline static
files with no build step.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
# then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser
```

WASD / arrows move · click to walk there · Shift sneak · E hold to crack the
vault · Space / right-click tosses a coin · P pause · M mute · R restart.
